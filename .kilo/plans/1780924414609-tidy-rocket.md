## Design rules
- Backgrounds: `bg-gray-100` for content pages, `bg-gray-800` for header/hero/footer
- Text: `text-gray-900` for headings, `text-gray-600` for body
- No glassmorphism, no bento grids, no 3D tilts, no scroll reveals, no animated counters
- Typography: Inter for body, Manrope for headings; keep font weights sensible
- Spacing: generous padding (`py-20` / `py-24`), `max-w-7xl mx-auto`, `px-4 sm:px-6 lg:px-8`
- Buttons: simple `bg-gray-800` or `bg-gray-700` with `text-white`, rounded-xl, no glow/shadow spam
- Lists: simple bullet points using standard `ul` / `li`, no custom icon pills
- Images: keep only logo and favicon in header/footer; remove hero background image overlays, map images, CTA backgrounds
